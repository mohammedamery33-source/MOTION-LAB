Choose a theme, reference images and the feeling you want the car to give.

STEP / 02

Set the priorities

Balance appearance, daily comfort, reliability and a realistic budget.

STEP / 03

Check the details

Verify fitment, safety, local rules and compatibility before buying parts.

STEP / 04

Build with intent

Make changes in a sensible order and keep the whole design cohesive.
